# Roles and What You Can Do

Access and visibility in CISA Campus Work Tracker reflect your role on campus. Pages throughout this Help section note when a topic applies only to specific roles.

## Full-timer (`admin`)
Full-timers carry administrative and team-wide oversight:
- Configure campus-wide settings, terms, and the Day's Goal.
- Pair Gospel Partners and manage team assignments.
- View team-wide metrics and unassigned contacts across the campus.
- Manage administrative data and system configurations.

## Trainee (`manager`)
Trainees are field workers active in day-to-day campus outreach:
- Manage assigned contacts and log interactions.
- Work together with a designated Gospel Partner for term outreach.
- Participate in The Journey board, follow-up queues, and team gatherings.

## Student (`operator`)
Student leaders and student workers assist with campus follow-ups and gatherings:
- Log conversations and follow-ups with contacts they are caring for.
- Check in attendees and track attendance at campus gatherings.
- Offer prayers and follow up on prayer requests.

## Community (`viewer`)
Community partners and supporters stay informed and aligned with the campus work:
- View permitted gathering announcements and schedules.
- Join in prayer burdens for the campus.
