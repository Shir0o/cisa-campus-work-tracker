# Getting Started

CISA Campus Work Tracker keeps a campus ministry's people, follow-ups, and gatherings in one place.

## What the app is for

- **People** records every contact you meet on campus.
- **Interactions** capture how each follow-up went.
- **Gatherings** and **Prayer** keep the team aligned week to week.

## Your role

What you can see and do follows your role: Full-timer, Trainee, Student, or Community. A page in this Help corpus that is meant for a specific role says so; otherwise it applies to everyone.
