# Notification Preferences

Configure how and when you receive alerts from CISA Campus Work Tracker to match your daily workflow.

## Notification Channels

1. **In-App Bell**: Displays active mentions, replies, and urgent follow-up items right inside the web navigation bar.
2. **Push Notifications**: Receive timely alerts on mobile for direct messages, urgent follow-up assignments, and scheduled reminders.

## Customizing Your Preferences

You can manage your notification subscriptions in **Settings > Notifications**:
- **Mentions & Threads**: Get notified whenever someone tags you (`@name`) or responds in an interaction thread you follow.
- **Assigned Follow-ups**: Receive alerts when you are assigned a contact or requested for follow-up care.
- **Weekly Reminders**: Choose whether to receive the weekly summary highlighting contacts not yet reached and upcoming team gatherings.
