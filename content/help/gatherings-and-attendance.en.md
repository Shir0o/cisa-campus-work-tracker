# Gatherings and Attendance

Gatherings track team meetings, Bible studies, and campus fellowship events.

## RSVPs and Attendance Flow

1. **Scheduled Gatherings**: Events appear on the Gatherings calendar with their target audience, location, and time.
2. **RSVPs**: Team members and invited students can indicate whether they plan to attend, helping organizers prepare materials and venue logistics.
3. **Live Check-in & Walk-ins**:
   - During the gathering, designated organizers or helpers check in attendees from the expected roster.
   - Walk-in guests can be added directly to attendance on the spot, creating a new contact or linking an existing record.
4. **Post-Gathering Wrap-up**:
   - Attendance logs feed into the contact's historical story, showing how consistently they participate.
   - The team can review who attended and reach out to those who were missed.
