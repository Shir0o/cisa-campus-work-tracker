# The Attention Feed and Bell

The Attention Feed (on My Day) and the notification bell keep you grounded in what needs attention right now without forcing you to dig through lists.

## "On you" vs. "Around the team"

The Attention Feed partitions updates into two distinct streams:

- **On you**: Items requiring your direct action or response. This includes questions waiting for your reply, assigned follow-up asks, and contacts in your care who have not been reached.
- **Around the team**: Important milestones, notes, and activity from teammates across the campus work. This gives you situational awareness of what others are experiencing without cluttering your personal to-do stack.

When "On you" says *"Nothing's waiting on you"*, your personal queue is clear. That is the ideal state—not a missing widget or broken layout.

## The Notification Bell

The bell icon in the navigation bar highlights new items requiring your attention since your last check:

- Unread mentions and direct follow-up requests.
- New answers to questions or updates on notes you authored.
- Important team announcements.

Clicking an item in the bell tray takes you directly to the relevant conversation, thread, or contact story.
