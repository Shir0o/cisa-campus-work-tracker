# Bible Study Reading Flow

The Bible Study tools help teams read scriptures, share outlines, and conduct studies together smoothly.

## Reading Deck and Document Index

- **Document Index**: Browse available reading schedules, series outlines, and study documents organized by term and topic.
- **Reading Deck**: Open a reading deck to follow along with the passage, footnotes, and discussion questions laid out for the session.
- **Bilingual Readings**: Passages and reading outlines support two-way English and Spanish views, allowing bilingual groups to read side by side.

## Present Mode

When presenting a Bible study in person or over a call:
- Enter **Present Mode** for a distraction-free, high-readability presentation screen.
- Move through verses, companion notes, and discussion points with simple keyboard shortcuts or click navigation.
