# Overview
Welcome to the latest release. This update reworks how partners and caring relationships are modelled, moves attendance onto the Gathering, and adds easier sharing.

## New Features
- Partners: founders are co-equal, and the current-term widening is retired
- Founders are recorded when a person is brought in by a pair, and existing contacts are backfilled
- Attendance moves onto the Gathering, with a staged intake and review
- Coordination docs can be shared with guests via a link
- [Mobile] Bible study copies a permanent per-week link, not just the latest
- Bible study renders the verse reference in accent color
- Tags suggest BFA and auto-remove the new-contact tag after five days

## UI/UX Updates
- Retain directory filters when returning from a contact detail
- Around-the-team surfaces public sign-up contacts to Full-timers, and keeps one worked-through conversation state
- [Mobile] A self-dismissed bottom sheet no longer re-dismisses

## Bug Fixes
- Contact caregiver field and care transfer are removed
- Taking someone into your sheep never grants reach; a carer tie ends with the tie that granted it
- The empty "This week" prayer eyebrow aligns with the spine dot
- Fixed an endless reload loop when a lazy chunk fails
