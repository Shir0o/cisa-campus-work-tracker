# Overview
Welcome to the latest release. This update makes contact collaboration safer and clearer, keeps your feed filters in place, and fixes a few crashes in the message queue.

## New Features
- Co-owners can manage a contact's collaborators, and an impersonation bypass is closed

## UI/UX Updates
- Around-the-team now keeps your filters and confirms when a comment posts
- [Mobile] Message cards in the queue are scoped to a full-timer's own people

## Bug Fixes
- [Mobile] Fixed a crash when a trainee's impersonated messages were read
