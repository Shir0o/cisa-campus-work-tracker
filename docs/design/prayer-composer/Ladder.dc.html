Issue #705 · why

rounded-xl is the roundest step in the app

Ink re-values three radius keys on @theme and leaves the rest of Tailwind’s scale at its defaults. Tailwind generates rounded-* from those same keys, so the overrides land on utilities that 534 existing call sites already used — and the ladder stops climbing.

src/index.css  L12–18
/* 14 interactive, 24 containers, 10 small marks,
   32 shell-level containers, 999 for pills. */
--radius-sm:   10px;   ← was 4
--radius:      14px;   ← new key
--radius-lg:   24px;   ← was 8
--radius-xl:   32px;   ← was 12
--radius-pill: 999px;  ← 0 uses

--radius-md, --radius-2xl and --radius-3xl are never named, so they stay at 6 / 16 / 24px. rounded-full does not read a key at all — it compiles to calc(infinity * 1px), which is why all 580 pills bypass --radius-pill.
