The half that has never worked

It reaches her phone, not just the app

sendPushNotification — the Expo path that reaches a phone with the app closed — is called from services/chat.ts and a Settings test button, and nowhere else. The bell’s sendNotification never calls it. On web, showWebPushNotification fires from inside an onSnapshot in a mounted component, so it only fires if the tab is already open.

So a question about a person has never reached anyone who was not already looking. Chat has proved the path works; this uses it. At most one push per contact per person per hour — two Full-timers going back and forth must not buzz three Trainees eight times. Everything still lands in the bell.

The card says “David asked you something”, not the generic line. attentionPhrase currently says that sentence for every thread item — notes, comments and encouragements included — which is why nobody trusts it.

How each one ends

Two kinds, two endings. A question ends when someone answers it; a follow-up ask ends only when a person says they did the thing. Nothing closes implicitly — not logging an Interaction, not replying.

A question ends by being answered

No resolve button and no status to maintain: the first reply from someone other than the asker answers it. That keeps it consistent with /questions, which decided deliberately that “asking and reading are ONE list and nothing is ever resolved”.

An Answered button exists for the case the reply happens in person — Ana tells David at Wednesday’s study — so the loop can be closed by hand without a fake message.

New: David sees his own unanswered questions. Today attention.ts:128 tracks unanswered questions from anyone else, so an asker can see the team’s outstanding questions and never their own. One inverted filter, and “both ends” stops being one end.

An ask is not an assignment

No owner and no due date — that is the whole difference between this and a to-do. A to-do is a personal list; putting someone else’s name and a date on an errand makes an obligation, and this is meant to say this wants doing to everyone tied and let someone pick it up.

The age is shown as a fact and nothing else: no colour shift, no reordering, no reminder. Drop it and an unowned ask becomes invisible, which is how “no obligation” turns into “nobody does it”. Escalating it would just be obligation with extra steps.

Anyone tied closes it with I followed up. David can retract it with Never mind — if he catches Tomas himself on Wednesday, the alternative is three Trainees carrying a dead ask or someone falsely claiming they did it.

Sam pressed the button. The card resolves on David’s screen, and Ana’s, and both keepers’, without any of them touching it. There is no per-person dismissal to track, because the thing tracked is the ask, not five people’s reading of it.

Closing is always explicit. Logging an Interaction with Tomas does not close it and neither does replying in the thread — you might have texted him about something else entirely, and silently closing David’s ask would lose it with nobody noticing.

It stays visible, resolved, until the date bucket rolls over. Vanishing the instant someone acts hides the useful half: that it was done, and by whom.

Four dead things this walks over — all of them readers with no writer, or branches with no caller

The Trainee’s feed has never run. attention.ts:92 gates on role === "trainee"; a Trainee is manager. Five months, no test, no execution — and had it run it would have handed every Trainee every Full-timer’s message about students they have never met. It is deleted, not revived: Trainee items now come from the four ties, the same set the notification uses, so the feed and the bell agree on who is involved.

The bell flattens every kind into “commented on” (threads.ts:270), because the one path that uses the kind-shaped titles is skipped for anyone already notified as a stakeholder. contact.reviewed has four readers and no writer outside the seed scripts — so the Trainee-facing “awaiting a look” could never resolve, and one of its branches would have rendered the literal string “undefined weighed in”. Both are recorded in DRIFT.md, rows 21–28.

The words on the contact page
Overview Interactions Follow-up Conversation Prayer Discussion Full-timers History

Follow-up → Conversation

A follow up is texting or emailing the contact after the first encounter. It was never a name for a place staff write — and the tab titled “Follow-up” describes itself, in its own subtitle, as “Comments on {name}”.

Interactions stays Interactions

The word appears 41 times in en.json, including the tab itself. Two stray strings say “conversations” six lines from "No interactions logged yet." — those are corrected, and mobile’s “Story” joins them.

Discussion → Full-timers

“Conversation” and “Discussion” are near-synonyms for two different audiences. The person who needs the distinction is the Full-timer, who sees both tabs and has to remember which one Ana can read. The label should carry the answer.
