Highlighting the ones with interactions

The feed already knows which stacks contain a conversation — stack.kinds has carried the set of item types since the day it was written, and the UI has never read it. What is missing is a signal, and the ladder below is drawn against the emphasis the card already has, not on top of an imagined one.

First, a finding — the existing emphasis does not render

A stack card marks itself unread with bg-surface border-outline-variant and read with bg-surface/60 border-outline-variant/40. Both sit inside a column card that is also bg-surface, so the fills are identical — the whole ladder rests on a 1px border of #F0F0F2 against #F4F4F5, which is 1.04:1. In light theme the two cards below are, to the eye, the same card.

What does carry unread today is the 8px accent dot beside the name (#52525B on #F4F4F5, 7.0:1) and the appearance of “Mark scanned”. Any interaction marker therefore has to stand on its own colour, because there is no working border ladder to step up from — and it must not be a border, or it inherits the same problem.

Both at once

The case that decides it. Unread is a grey dot; talked is a tinted pill with a word. They occupy the same row and do not compete, because they are different shapes saying different things — new to you and someone has been with them.

Order is fixed: name, dot, chip. Never the reverse — the dot belongs to the name.

What is not proposed, and why

Not a border, not a background, not a shadow: the card already spends all three on unread and gets nothing back for them. Not a coloured left rail on its own — without a word it reads as a stage colour, and the stages already own that vocabulary on The Journey. Not a count (“2 conversations”): the summary line under the name already says who logged what, so a number would repeat it.

The border ladder, separately

Fixing the inert unread border is a real change and a different one — moving unread to --outline (#E4E4E7, 1.16:1) barely helps; it wants a surface step, and that is a token conversation, not a feed one. Worth its own row in DRIFT.md either way. The marker above is drawn so it does not depend on that being settled first.
