The filter row

Two controls, in the clothes Looking back already wears — the same pill group and the same person select, so the two feeds read as siblings rather than as two people's ideas. No search field: the feed is already stacked per person and cut off at five, and a third control would earn nothing.

Anatomy, at 1:1

Every value here is lifted from History.tsx:419–451. Nothing is new but the labels.

13px / 6px×12px / 999px. Selected takes --background and --on-surface; the rest are --on-surface-variant.

40px tall, 12px inset, 999px, --surface on --outline-variant. Border goes to --primary on focus and while set.
Row
12px gap, wraps. Sits under the title, above both columns — one row governs the whole feed, not each column.
