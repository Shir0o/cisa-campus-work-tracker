Two teams, and the names are yours

“YP” and “Campus” are drawn as the two the feedback named, and the tally says numbers vary — but nothing here assumes two. If a third term brings a third team, this list grows; the chip row on the feed grows with it and wraps, which is why the row is drawn as a wrapping pill track rather than a fixed three-up control.

Pairs and teams are independent

A pair sits inside one team in every case drawn here, because that is how the team was described. Nothing enforces it, and nothing needs to: the feed filter reads the person's team, never the pair's, so a pair that straddles two teams simply shows up under both chips. Leaving them uncoupled means a pair can be re-cut mid-term without disturbing the teams.

What this costs to build

One field on the user document, and the same field added to the firestore.rules user allowlist — without that second line every write is rejected and the picker silently does nothing. Both copies of the roster helper need it. Admin-only, like the pairs beside it.
