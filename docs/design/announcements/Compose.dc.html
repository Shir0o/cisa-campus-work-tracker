Sending an announcement

Today an announcement is opened from the second tab of New message and addressed by ticking people out of the full user list, one at a time. Then it is posted with the same composer as a direct message, and it arrives on 24 phones titled “New message”. Three changes: an audience you can name, a compose step that admits it is a broadcast, and a notification that says what it is.

Change one

Address it to a group, not to a list of people

The audience presets were in the mobile v2 design and were dropped in the port because the roster data wasn’t there — CreateChatModal.tsx:12–18 says so. Two of the three presets need no roster: “the whole app” is every approved user, which CreateChatModal already queries. The per-team presets need the team field that news-filters proposes for #727 and which is not built — AppUser has uid, email, displayName, photoURL, approved, role and nothing else. Ship those two first if #727 slips. Hand-picking stays, as the last option.

Nothing remembers the audience. A weekly announcement to the same 24 people is 24 taps every week, and a missed tick is silent — the person simply never sees it.

A preset is stored as the rule, not as the 24 uids it resolves to today. Someone who joins next week is in the audience without anyone editing the channel — which is the behaviour the copy already promises (“Everyone here reads it”). It also means the room’s memberIds stop being the source of truth for who reads it, which firestore.rules currently uses to gate reads: the rule has to resolve to a member list somewhere, and that is the part to design before building.

Change two

A compose step, not a chat line

A one-line textarea with a round send button is the right shape for “ok, see you at 7”. It is the wrong shape for something 24 people are about to be pushed. The Announcement tab gets its own body field and a review step that states the size of the audience before it goes.

Change three

Stop calling it “New message”

sendMessage (services/chat.ts:218–251) notifies every recipient with the title “New message” and the body “Name: text”, whatever kind of room it is. So the one thing in the app that is meant to be read by everybody arrives looking exactly like a note from a friend, and gets triaged like one.

Today
New message 2h Naomi Kwan: Reading week moves the Thursday gathering to the Chaplaincy lounge, 7pm…

Indistinguishable from a direct message, and stacked with them in the bell. The only announcement-shaped notification the app sends today is the one fired when the channel is created — which is the least important moment in its life.

Proposed
Campus updates 2h Naomi posted an announcement: Reading week moves the Thursday gathering to the Chaplaincy lounge, 7pm…

Titled with the channel, bodied with what changed. One extra branch in sendMessage on the room type it already fetches to build the recipient list — no new read, and it also fixes the in-app bell, which shares the same call.
