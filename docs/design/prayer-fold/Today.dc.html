The line coloring

Four line jobs, four unrelated values. Contrast against the card, #161618:

{{l.name}} {{l.token}} {{l.ratio}}

This week's 2px rail is the loudest object on the page — louder than the person's name. The other rails and all three eyebrow rules sit at 1.15:1, which is nothing at all. Light inverts it exactly: a #131316 rail at 16.9:1 on a #F4F4F5 card, and #F0F0F2 rules at 1.03:1.

A hairline is meant to be quiet, so 1.15:1 is not the bug on its own. The bug is the spread: one line value in this card is doing sixteen times the work of the others, because the rail is being asked to say where you are and how it landed at once.

Crammed

Opening the fold adds 396px for four prayers you are only glancing at — 85px each. Of that, 144px is a Mark chip row printed four times for history nobody is marking. (Edit and Make a to-do ride the date line, so they cost no height — they are just noise on it.)

The four entries have no gap and no divider between them — one entry's chip row butts straight into the next entry's date. That is the crammed.

Card total, fold open: ≈824px — 24 + 56 + 20 + 132 + 20 + 132 + 20 + 396 + 24 — taller than the whole 662px window it was reported from.

Two line systems, crossed

Three full-width horizontal rules, one per section, run across three vertical rails that each start and stop under them. Neither reads as structure; together they read as noise. The fold's ▶ is a 9px text glyph, so it sits off the baseline of the 11px label beside it.
