The rule

Lines are structure. Colour is state.

Today a line does both jobs and does neither well: the thread's vertical rail is asked to say where you are and how the prayer landed, so it swings from 17.3:1 to 1.15:1 down one card. Split the jobs. One neutral rail carries the structure at a single value; a 9px dot on it carries the state.

1 · One line value, both themes

Every structural line in the card — the card edge and the spine — becomes --outline, the value the app already uses for real dividers. --outline-variant stops being used for structure and keeps its one honest job, the rim on an unselected mark chip, where it is meant to be barely there. Be clear about the size of the win: this moves a hairline from 1.15 to 1.27 dark, 1.04 to 1.15 light. Both are still quiet, and that is correct — a hairline should be. The change that matters is the 2px rail leaving at 17.3:1, not the hairline getting darker.

2 · The dot alphabet

Nine pixels of colour instead of a 2×90px bar — about a third of the ink, and it lands on the spine where the eye is already travelling. Status fills the dot. This week rings it. Those are two independent facts today's rail tries to say with one value, which is why an ongoing prayer from this week and an answered one from August currently look like different kinds of object.

3 · The rhythm

Everything on a 4px step, and the two rules that were doing the separating are gone — the spine separates now.

The section eyebrows keep their words but lose their rules: THIS WEEK becomes 10.5px/600 uppercase, tracked, sitting inline with its date. That is where the differentiation the 11px grey label had lost comes back from — the label is doing the work the hairline was failing to do.

An earlier prayer collapses to one 30px line — dot, date, mark, the burden truncated. Click it and it opens in place with its text and its actions. The Mark chips and the Edit / to-do row stop being printed four times for history nobody is editing.

5 · Two things this touches beyond the fold
The card edge

The card's own border is --outline-variant too, so it is invisible in both themes and each card floats with no edge. Moving it to --outline with the spine is one word and it is the same complaint. Called out separately because it is a page-wide change, not a fold change.

Mobile has the same fold

PrayerListMobile.tsx carries the same ▶ glyph (at 10px), the same three eyebrow rules and the same three rail colours at pl-3.5 border-l-2. It is not identical — two of its rules are /60, and its earlier list has space-y-2, so the no-gap cramming is desktop-only. The line colouring is the same bug on both; the cramming is worse here.
