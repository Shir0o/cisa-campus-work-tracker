I
Design system · replaces Bento

Ink

Monochrome shell, colour only in data. Bento's violet stops carrying meaning and black takes over as the accent; selection becomes inversion rather than a tint. Same token names, same 4,254 utility classes — new values.

The four moves

everything else is downstream of these
01

Surfaces invert

Bento is white cards on a grey page. Ink is grey cards on a white page. Swapping --bg and --panel retints every card at once, with no markup touched.

02

Borders give way to tint

Cards separate by fill, not by line. --border survives only for controls that need an outline — inputs, ghost buttons, dividers.

03

Selection is inversion

With no violet, a tinted background no longer reads as “active”. The selected thing fills solid black and its content goes white.

04

Colour retreats into data

Stage and board hues are now the only chroma on screen, so their chroma goes up, not down. Everything structural is neutral.
