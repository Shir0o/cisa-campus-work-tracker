What to look at

  • 01Panels 1–3 settle. Content fits, so it centres and wears the card — the slack becomes margin around an object instead of a void beneath one.
  • 02Switch to Light. The card is now lighter than its ground and carries a real three-layer shadow, so it reads as an object in both themes rather than a grey patch on white.
  • 03Panel 4 overflows, and that is fine — it scrolls. Card grows is settled; the toggle is left in so the rejected branch stays visible.
  • 04The counter and rail track the scroll. Driven by the IntersectionObserver that is dead in the shipped reader.
Light and dark elevate differently. The app's light theme sets --shadow-card: none and fills cards with --panel on a white --bg — so the card came out darker than its ground, which reads as a well rather than an object. Here the pair is swapped: ground takes --panel, the card takes --bg, and light elevates through a three-layer shadow while dark keeps elevating through lightness and an edge. Same tokens, no new colours — but a deliberate departure from the app's flat light-mode card.
