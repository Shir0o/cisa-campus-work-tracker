Peace that holds

Week 4 · Thursday, September 10
01

Where peace starts

  • Peace with God is a standing, not a mood.
  • The access we have was , never earned.
  • What we stand in now is what we will stand in at the end.
Discuss

Where do you catch yourself treating peace with God as a feeling that comes and goes?

02

What suffering is doing

  • Suffering is not the opposite of hope — it is the to it.
  • Character is not given. It is produced.
Activity

In pairs, two minutes each. Name one thing you are enduring right now, and one thing it has already produced in you.

03

Hope that does not shame

  • Hope is safe because of who it, not how strongly we hold it.
Question

What would change this week if your hope did not depend on how you felt on Tuesday?
