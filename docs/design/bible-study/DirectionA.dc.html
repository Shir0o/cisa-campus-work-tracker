Peace that holds

Week 4 of 12 · 3 sections
1

Where peace starts

  • Peace with God is a standing, not a mood.
  • The access we have was , never earned.
  • What we stand in now is what we will stand in at the end.

Being therefore justified by faith, we have peace with God through our Lord Jesus Christ; through whom we also have our access by faith into this grace in which we stand. We rejoice in hope of the glory of God.

Romans 5:1–2 · WEB
Discuss

Where do you catch yourself treating peace with God as a feeling that comes and goes?

2

What suffering is doing

  • Suffering is not the opposite of hope — it is the to it.
  • Character is not given. It is produced.

We also rejoice in our sufferings, knowing that suffering produces perseverance; and perseverance, proven character; and proven character, hope.

Romans 5:3–4 · WEB
Activity

In pairs, two minutes each. Name one thing you are enduring, and one thing it has produced.

3

Hope that does not shame

  • Hope is safe because of who it, not how strongly we hold it.
Question

What would change this week if your hope did not depend on how you felt on Tuesday?
