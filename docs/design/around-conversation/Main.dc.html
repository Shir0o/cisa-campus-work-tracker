Reading what was said, without leaving

Around the team tells you that something happened and never what was said. Both ways out of that are broken: going to read it costs you your filters, and writing back produces no evidence it worked. The page subscribes to every conversation in the product and renders none of them — it has been a write-only surface. One control fixes both: the card opens the person's Conversation in place.

1 · Collapsed, and the dot is gone

The accent dot meant unseen. With one state left, every un-reviewed card would carry one — a dot on everything marks nothing. The count it was standing in for moves onto the control that earns it.

One control, not a new one

Comment becomes Conversation · 3 — the same button, now carrying the count and toggling the strip instead of swapping itself for a composer. Nothing is added to the action row; the dot is removed from the header.

When there is nothing yet

No messages, no number: the button reads Comment, exactly as it does today, and opens onto an empty strip with the composer ready. The count appears only when there is something to count.

What the dot was really doing

It marked unseen, and unseen is being retired here (ADR 0022). Dropping it is not a loss of information — it is the removal of a second marker for a state the page no longer keeps.

Height is unchanged

Collapsed cards keep the two-line body clamp and the same anatomy, so the 3-up grid at xl still tiles evenly. Everything new lives below the fold of the card.

2 · Open — and the clamp comes off

#965 says the body is unreadable because it is clipped at two lines. That clamp exists to keep collapsed cards a uniform height. Once you open a card you have left the grid's uniformity behind, so the same gesture releases both clips: the interaction text unclamps and the Conversation appears under it.

Messages above, composer below

The composer sits at the foot of the strip and the list grows toward it, so a posted message appears directly above where it was typed — the shortest possible distance between the act and its evidence.

Both threads, as two tabs

A contact has two: Conversation, open to everyone tied to them, and Full-timers, staff-only. The strip carries both, the same pair and the same order as the contact page. Around is Full-timer-only, so there is no role branch here — the second tab is always permitted on this page.

The button names the tab it opens

Conversation · 3 opens onto Conversation and its three messages; Full-timers · 2 sits beside it. No new collective noun for the pair — the product does not have one, and inventing one here would be a fifth word for something already named twice.

Reuse, don't rebuild

This is Thread.tsx in its existing compact variant, not a new renderer. Same mentions, same reactions, same kind picker the contact page uses — and the same scope argument that already separates the two tabs there.

Unclamping is the fix for #965

"Some fields are hard to read because of length" is the two-line clamp. Opening the card releases it in the same click that reveals the conversation — you never leave the page to read a long note.

Still does not move

An open card grows in place. It does not re-date, re-sort, or jump to another day heading — the day it sits under means when the team touched this person, not when someone last remarked on it.

One at a time

Opening a second card closes the first. Two expanded cards in a 3-up grid leave a ragged hole, and the page is for working down a list, not for holding several conversations open at once.

3 · The instant you post — all of #966

Today the composer closes, the accent dot disappears, and the message renders nowhere. The only feedback for commenting is the card getting dimmer. Here the evidence is the message itself, landing where you were already looking.

Today

The write succeeds. addThreadMessage reaches Firestore.
The composer unmounts and the draft clears.
markSeen fires — the accent dot disappears.
The "Posted" toast is a no-op: the page is mounted with no props, so onToast is undefined.
The message never renders — thread items on untied contacts are dropped before they become anything.

Net: you write something, the composer vanishes, the card gets quieter, and nothing else happens.

Proposed

The message appears at the foot of the strip, an inch below where you typed it.
The composer stays open and empty — you may have more to say.
The count on the button ticks 3 → 4.
The revived snackbar confirms it, as a second signal rather than the only one.
Nothing is marked reviewed. The card does not dim, re-date, or move.

The evidence that a message posted is the message. Everything else is corroboration.

Why the composer stays open

Closing it was half of why the post felt like it evaporated — the thing you were looking at disappeared and nothing took its place. Leaving it open and empty says "that went through, carry on" without a word of copy.

The fresh message is marked, briefly

A tinted block and Just now in the success tone for a few seconds, then it settles into an ordinary row. Enough to catch the eye that was on the Post button; not a permanent badge.

The snackbar already exists

The page renders UndoSnackbar and holds useUndoSnack already — the composer's toast simply routes to a prop nobody passes. That wiring lands in the small correctness PR, ahead of this design.

Encouragements too

The ❤️ popover posts a canned encouragement and calls the same dead toast. It gets the same snackbar — but stays out of the strip, summarised rather than listed, as it is everywhere else.
